# Article Catalog — portabel.id

## Scope and boundaries

This catalog expands the evidence-led authority map in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md` into article briefs for `portabel.id`. It covers portable/modular facilities and the broader product families already present on the homepage, but gives stronger evidence gates to occupied, transported, lifted, powered, fuelled, plumbed, accessible, sanitary, and medically regulated systems.

Planned neutral articles use `/artikel/<slug>/`. Current products, availability, prices, rental/service scope, quotations, and geography belong to separate verified commercial routes. Articles do not replace engineering, clinical advice, manufacturer instructions, permits, qualified trade work, or local approval. Cannibalization is assessed only within `portabel.id`; related owned domains may independently cover the same subject.
