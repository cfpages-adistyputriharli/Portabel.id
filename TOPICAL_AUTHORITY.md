# Topical Authority — portabel.id

## Role and boundary

`portabel.id` should become an Indonesian reference for deciding, specifying, moving, installing, operating, and retiring portable products. The existing homepage makes the domain broader than portable buildings: it names consumer appliances, electronics, outdoor equipment, furniture, medical devices, temporary utilities, event structures, kiosks, modular rooms, and sanitary units. The authority plan preserves that evidence, but gives engineering depth to occupied, lifted, transported, fuelled, powered, plumbed, or medically regulated products where a bad decision has larger consequences.

The domain's primary audience is Indonesian owners, buyers, renters, facility teams, event and project operators, designers, contractors, technicians, and consumers who need to distinguish genuine mobility from a marketing label. Neutral education belongs under planned `/artikel/<slug>/` routes. Commercial product, rental, service, quote, and location intent must remain on separate verified product/service routes; educational pages may explain requirements and link to those routes without becoming disguised sales pages.

This plan does not replace project-specific structural calculations, a lift plan signed off by competent parties, route and transport permits, PBG/SLF and local approvals, fire engineering, electrical/plumbing design, medical advice, or a manufacturer's instructions. Exact capacities, dimensions, code thresholds, servicing ratios, and clinical claims may be published only from current primary documents or verified product data. Location-swapped pages are excluded unless local climate, access, regulation, disaster risk, or a documented project creates substantively different evidence.

Related Syamsul-managed domains may independently cover the same subject. That is not cannibalization: the anti-cannibalization boundary in this plan applies only to URLs on `portabel.id`.

The dominant SEO risk is not missing keyword variants; it is publishing thin product-name pages without distinct reader jobs. The dominant user-safety risk is presenting “portable” as evidence of safe transport, rapid installation, medical suitability, weather resistance, or code exemption. Portability changes interfaces and lifecycle risks; it does not remove them.

## Internal-link architecture

- `/artikel/portabel-modular-arti-jenis-dan-batasnya/` is the central knowledge hub (`POR-01-A01`). It links to each parent-topic hub, while each child links upward to its topic hub and the central hub.
- The decision path is `POR-01` vocabulary → `POR-02` brief/site → `POR-18` procurement. Product and service routes receive links only from relevant decision pages after their scope is verified.
- The physical-delivery path is `POR-03` structure → `POR-05` transport → `POR-06` lift → `POR-07` foundation → `POR-08` installation/handover → `POR-17` maintenance → `POR-19` relocation/end of life.
- The occupied-facility path is `POR-04` envelope → `POR-09` utilities → `POR-10` fire/life safety → `POR-11` accessibility → `POR-12` sanitation.
- Diagnostic pages in `POR-17` link to prevention/inspection, the applicable system owner, and repair-versus-retirement decisions in `POR-19`; they do not funnel every symptom directly to a sales route.
- `POR-13`, `POR-15`, and `POR-16` use family-specific lateral links. Event power links to `POR-09` and `POR-10`; a wheelchair guide links to accessibility in `POR-11`; consumer-gas content links to fuel/fire boundaries in `POR-10`.
- Application patterns live inside the selection journey in `POR-02` and relevant family articles. They summarize changed constraints and link to technical owners rather than duplicating mini-guides.
- Related links are chosen from each row's `Related IDs`; no fixed portfolio link block is copied across articles. Orphan checks are mandatory before every publication wave.

## Evidence and editorial standards

Use the following sources as evidence registers, not permission to quote inaccessible clauses or assume that one standard applies to every portable product:

- [PP No. 16 Tahun 2021 on building implementation](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/161846/pp-no-16) was listed as in force when audited. It establishes building safety, structural load, fire, electrical/lightning, accessibility, and approval context. The project location and building status still determine applicability.
- The official BSN catalog listed [SNI 1726:2019 for seismic design](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/12762-sni17262019), [SNI 1727:2020 for minimum design loads](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/12927-sni17272020), and [SNI 1729:2020 for structural steel buildings](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/12882-sni17292020) as in force during the audit. Structural articles require a qualified engineer to select the applicable standards and edition.
- The BSN catalog includes PUIL parts for ordinary and special locations, including construction/demolition, exhibitions/stands, temporary entertainment installations, wet locations, medical locations, and solar systems. A 2025 revision already exists for shock protection, demonstrating why authors must check the [current PUIL catalog](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/index?key=Puil) rather than copy an old edition.
- The BSN catalog showed both 2015 and 2025 plumbing entries during this audit. Plumbing authors must resolve the applicable edition in the [current SNI catalog](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/index?key=8153) and obtain the actual standard; search snippets are not design authority.
- [Permen PUPR No. 14/PRT/M/2017](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/104477/permen-) was listed as in force for building accessibility. Accessibility content still requires current local implementation, complete facility review, and appropriate user input.
- [Permenaker No. 8 Tahun 2020](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/1668/peraturan-menteri-nomor-8-tahun-2020) was listed as in force for occupational safety of lifting and transport equipment. It is a legal review gate, not a substitute for a project lift plan.
- [Permenhub No. 60 Tahun 2019](https://jdih.dephub.go.id/peraturan/index?kelompok=3&page=9&per-page=12&style=row) and [Permenhub No. 18 Tahun 2021](https://jdih.dephub.go.id/peraturan/detail?data=GBUIJXJHQreL2hAq5gFYEV4knwM5RBRIm4jul0pPV2Yq4pEWymPazb84Pb1qzyn5Xv8MLhagVFuJl4uTPjU4YaF34fTx1JOOQqP4pAvNAXPHqzKR2NhRHX6dz5tU0oII8CYREYz2NbBEbrCEZZS2DwtgKV) provide current-audit starting points for goods transport and load supervision. A licensed carrier and current route-specific approval remain necessary.
- [Permenkes No. 2 Tahun 2023](https://jdih.kemkes.go.id/pdfjs/web/viewer.html?file=https%3A%2F%2Fjdih.kemkes.go.id%2Fstorage%2Fdocuments%2Fpdfs%2F2023permenkes002.pdf) addresses environmental-health media and facilities, but [Permenkes No. 3 Tahun 2026](https://jdih.kemkes.go.id/documents/peraturan-menteri-kesehatan-nomor-3-tahun-2026) partially changed its provisions during this audit. Sanitation content must verify the consolidated current rule before publishing thresholds.
- The official [Kemenkes Regalkes system](https://regalkes.kemkes.go.id/) is the starting point for medical-device registration evidence. Registration, intended use, manufacturer instructions, distributor status, service/calibration evidence, and clinical review are separate checks.

Editorial rules:

- Distinguish verified fact, calculation input, professional judgment, product claim, and hypothesis in both copy and references.
- Use exact standards or regulations only after checking title, status, edition, applicability, amendments, local implementation, and access to the authoritative text on the publication date.
- Every structural, lift, transport, fire, electrical, gas/fuel, sanitation, accessibility, or medical brief receives a named competent reviewer before publication.
- Never invent dimensions, capacities, wind limits, lifting points, service intervals, toilet ratios, prices, customer numbers, test results, certification, case studies, or firsthand experience.
- Consumer “how-to” pages may cover inspection and low-risk operation within manufacturer instructions. They must include stop conditions and may not teach live electrical work, rigging, structural modification, fuel improvisation, waste discharge, or clinical treatment.
- Comparison tables must normalize the same use case and evidence. “Portable”, “premium”, “medical grade”, “fireproof”, “weatherproof”, “eco-friendly”, and “maintenance-free” are claims to test, not accepted categories.
- Original photos require provenance and rights records. Real case studies need dates, site constraints, consent, specifications, actual process, defects/limitations, and measured results.
- Refresh legal and standards evidence at drafting and again at publication; this 2026 audit is not a permanent compliance opinion.
